Stridr Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Stridr ("the app", "we") is an activity tracker for running, riding, walking and hiking. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. Stridr is built on-device first: you can use it fully as a guest with no account, and your activity data stays on your phone unless you choose to sign in.

Data we collect

  • Location (precise, foreground & background). While you are recording an activity, the app collects GPS location to draw your route, distance, pace and elevation. With your consent, it continues recording even when the app is closed or the screen is off, so a run isn't lost when your phone locks. Location is only collected while a recording is active.
  • Activities you save. Route, time, distance, elevation, sport type, title and the date of each activity.
  • Photos you attach. If you add photos to an activity, the selected images are stored with that activity. The app only accesses images you explicitly pick.
  • Account info (optional). If you sign in, we process your email address to authenticate you, plus a display name you can edit. You can sign in with an email and password, or with Google Sign-In — in which case Google shares your email address and basic profile (name) with the app to create your account. We never receive your Google password.
  • Social content (optional, when signed in). Kudos, comments, follows, segments, challenges and clubs you create or join.
  • Basic device/app info. Information needed to run and update the app, such as app version and over-the-air update checks.

How your data is stored and shared

  • On your device. As a guest, your saved activities are stored locally on your phone and are not uploaded to our backend.
  • In your account (only if you sign in). Your activities, profile and social content sync to our backend provider, Supabase, so you can access them across devices. Row-level security is used to control access to your data and public content.
  • Sign-in with Google (optional). If you choose Google Sign-In, authentication is handled by Google, which verifies your identity and returns your email and basic profile to create your account. Google's handling of that sign-in is governed by Google's own privacy policy.
  • Maps. Map tiles are served by OpenStreetMap / CARTO. Loading a map sends standard web requests, which may include your IP address, to those tile servers, as with other websites and map services.
  • We do not sell your data, and Stridr contains no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Why we need background location

Background location is used solely to keep recording your route when the app is in the background or the screen is locked during an activity. You are shown an in-app explanation before the permission is requested, and you can decline and record with the app open only. You can revoke location access at any time in your device Settings.

Your choices & rights

  • Use the app as a guest with no account and no cloud storage for your activity data.
  • Delete any activity in the app; deletions propagate to your account if you're signed in.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us below.
  • Revoke location, photo or other permissions in your device Settings.

Children

Stridr is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
